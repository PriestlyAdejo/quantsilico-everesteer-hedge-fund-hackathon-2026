## Operating rules

<Callout tone="info">
Only integrity failures are universal hard stops. Soft research metrics guide
budget; they do not invent official scores.
</Callout>

<MetricDefinition name="Submission mode">
DISABLED blocks uploads, DRY_RUN records evidence without Everesteer upload,
ARMED allows real practice/live submits after human intent.
</MetricDefinition>
